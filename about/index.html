About Alora

For women, by women.

Where dreams meet light.

Alora was born from a love for beautiful things, creativity and the little details that make life special.

Established in 2025 as Jolie Femme, what began as a small South African business grew into something much bigger than we first imagined. It became a dream of bringing together beauty, creativity, thoughtful gifting and everyday luxuries in one place.

Today, that dream continues under a new name: Alora — where dreams meet light.

Our name has changed, our vision has grown, but the heart behind everything remains the same.

A little something for everyone.

We believe that beauty can be found in the simplest things. A little self-care after a long day, a beautiful journal waiting to be filled, a personalised gift chosen with love, or something special that simply makes you smile.

At Alora, we bring together a thoughtfully curated collection of beauty and pampering essentials, jewellery, stationery, creative treasures, home accessories, faith-inspired gifts, personalised products and beautifully assembled gift sets.

Beauty & pamperJewelleryPress-onsStationeryHomeFaith giftsGift sets

Whether you're looking to spoil yourself, surprise someone special, celebrate a milestone or add a little luxury to your everyday routine, we want you to find something that feels just right.

Thoughtfully chosen. Lovingly created.

We have a passion for creativity, meaningful details and making ordinary things extraordinary.

Some of our products are carefully selected, while others are lovingly personalised or put together with a little extra thought. From custom keepsakes and thoughtful teacher gifts to beautiful gift boxes and creative kits, we believe that the best gifts are the ones that make someone feel remembered.

More than a shop. A dream in bloom.

Alora represents growth, new beginnings and the courage to turn little dreams into something beautiful.

We are proud to be a South African small business, built on passion, creativity and the desire to bring a little more beauty into everyday life.

Thank you for being part of our journey, supporting our dream and allowing us to be part of your special moments.
