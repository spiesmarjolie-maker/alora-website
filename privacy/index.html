Privacy notice

How Alora handles your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you order or create an account we collect your name, email address, phone number and delivery address, and what you ordered. If you join our mailing list we keep your email address.

Why we collect it

Only to process and deliver your order, send you order updates, help you with questions, and (if you asked) send our news. We never sell or rent your details.

Who we share it with

Payfast processes your payment; we never see or store your card details. PUDO receives your name, phone number and delivery details to deliver your parcel. Our web host stores the site's data in South Africa.

Cookies and statistics

We use one cookie to remember your bag and login. Our visit statistics only count page views per day; they do not identify or track you. Our fonts and scripts are served from our own site, so no third party sees your visit.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Contact us at hello.alora97@gmail.com or on 081 737 1752.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for five years for tax purposes. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete your account.
